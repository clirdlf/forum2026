Thank you to everyone who participated in Community Voting for the 2026 Virtual DLF Forum\! We appreciate the hundreds of DLF community members who dedicated time to reviewing proposals and providing thoughtful feedback. Your engagement directly informs the Program Committee’s work and helps us create a program that represents the interests, needs, and expertise of our community.

With Community Voting now complete, the Program Committee will spend the coming week carefully considering proposal evaluations, community feedback, and overall program balance as they make final selections for this year’s Forum.

### Upcoming Milestones

- **June 8:** Program Committee meets to finalize program selections.
- **June 23:** The 2026 Virtual DLF Forum program is released, registration opens, and applications launch for the 2026 Digital Storytelling Fellows program (more soon\!).

We look forward to sharing the program with you later this month and to celebrating the inspiring work happening across the DLF community this October.
